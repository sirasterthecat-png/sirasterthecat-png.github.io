StreamCut Studio✧

Create it. Schedule it. See how it performs.

StreamCut Studio is an all-in-one content-production and management workspace in development. It aims to bring video editing, thumbnail creation, post scheduling and buffering, consolidated comments, and performance statistics into a single application. From editing a video to managing its release and understanding audience engagement, the goal is to keep the entire creator workflow together.

WHAT'S TAKING SHAPE

Where the focus is.

01 / FOCUS AREAS

Create videos and thumbnails, prepare content for publication, and understand audience response—all from one connected workspace. These are development goals; platform availability and integration details are still being determined.

01 / VIDEO

Video editor

Edit recordings, clips and Shorts with planned review, captions, composition and rendering tools in the same application.

02 / THUMBNAIL

Thumbnail editor

Create and refine thumbnails next to the videos they accompany, without moving every design into a separate application.

03 / WORKSPACE

Keep every asset together

Connect editing projects, thumbnails and publishing details so the pieces of one video remain organized in one workspace.

04 / BUFFER

Buffer and schedule posts

Prepare content in advance and manage a posting queue across supported publishing destinations, subject to platform authorization.

05 / COMMENTS

Consolidate comments

Bring comments from supported connected platforms into one place for efficient review and response planning.

06 / STATS

Consolidate performance stats

See relevant content and channel performance statistics across supported connected services without switching among dashboards.

THE IDEA BEHIND IT

A single workspace from creation to audience.

Content production should not end at export. StreamCut Studio aims to bring video editing, thumbnail design, post buffering, comments and performance statistics into one application. Its publishing tools will remain permission-based, and which platforms can be connected will depend on their supported interfaces.

DEVELOPMENT NOTES

Still on the workbench.

  • The goal is an integrated video and thumbnail editor with post buffering, unified comments and consolidated stats.
  • Supported connections, posting permissions and available analytics depend on each platform.
  • Free and open-source distribution is planned; no public build or source-code release has been announced.
  • StreamCut Studio is a working name, and the release timing may change.
